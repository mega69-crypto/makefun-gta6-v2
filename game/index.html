SOLARA BAY
A SUN-SOAKED OPEN-WORLD CRIME GAME

You are Vic's new wheelman in Solara Bay, a coastal city that never sleeps. Steal cars, outrun the cops, run Vic's jobs, and take the Big Score. The bay is yours if you can hold it.

WASD / Arrows — walk & drive  ·  Shift — run
E — steal / exit car, save at the Hideout  ·  Space — punch / handbrake
Mouse + Click — aim & shoot (drive-by from a car)  ·  Esc — pause  ·  M — mute
Progress saves at the Hideout. Wanted stars fade when you stay hidden — or repaint at Pay N Spray.
